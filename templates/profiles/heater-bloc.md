Heater Bloc's has produced a guide for building a Copper Coil Alcohol Heater and Safety Enclosure, which is a small alcohol-based heater that can be made at home. The idea is that these are useful light sources, heaters and even stoves for people experiencing homelessness. The product is a 20-page set of instructions.

- **Project maintainer:** Heater Bloc
- **Collaborative space:** The [Heater Bloc page on X](https://x.com/HeaterBloc) (formerly Twitter). You can make suggestions or link to improvements you've made to the materials.
- **Needs Doing list**: Possibly comments made on the X page.
- **Adapting the project:** You will need to adapt the project to the materials you can get in your area (though they are fairly common and simple) and the tools you have available. You can contribute what you've learned back to the community, but they will have to decide whether they want to make changes to the instructions.
