The Hovalin is an open source, 3D printable acoustic violin that you can make yourself or have the project maintainers make for you (to buy). It can be produced using most standard consumer 3d printers and is available in 4/4, 3/4 and 1/2 sizes. The total cost of raw materials for the violin (4/4) is currently about $70. The Hovalin was designed with Autodesk Fusion 360, and its shape and dimensions are inspired by the Stradivarius violin model.

- **Project maintainer:** Hova Labs
- **Collaborative space:** The [hovalin_community](https://www.reddit.com/r/hovalin_community/) subreddit on Reddit. You can upload modified files to the project maintainers.
- **Needs Doing list**: Not sure if they have one, but there may be discussions about that on the subreddit.
- **Adapting the project:** When you download the project files, you have options for different sizes and printer types. By printing and assembling the project using your specific tool set, you are adapting it. If you have challenges specific to your set up, you can contribute what you've learned back to the community.
