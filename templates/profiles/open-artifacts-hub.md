The Open Artifacts Hub is an online database (this website) with information about existing open artifacts projects. Our mission is to make it easier for cultural creators to find work that needs doing as part of their creative practice.

- **Project maintainer:** Jim Applegate
- **Collaborative space:** The [Github Project](https://github.com/jimappleg8/open-artifacts-hub). You can contribute by making a change request.
- **Needs Doing list:** The [Github Issues Queue](https://github.com/jimappleg8/open-artifacts-hub/issues).
- **Adapting the project:** This data is free to use per the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license.
