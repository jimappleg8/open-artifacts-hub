Open Source Ecology (OSE) is a collaborative network of farmers, engineers, and supporters dedicated to developing the Global Village Construction Set (GVCS)—a collection of 50 open-source industrial machines designed to enable the creation of sustainable and resilient communities.

- **Project maintainer:** Marcin Jakubowski, Ph.D., founded OSE in 2003 and continues to lead the organization. (wiki.opensourceecology.org)

- **Collaborative space:** OSE engages with its community through various platforms, including their official website, wiki, and social media channels. They also host workshops and training programs to facilitate hands-on collaboration.

- **Needs Doing list:** OSE maintains a GVCS Tools Status page on their wiki, outlining the development stages of each machine and areas where contributions are needed. Additionally, the Getting Involved page provides information on current opportunities for participation.

- **Adapting the project:** OSE's designs are released under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License, allowing individuals to adapt and modify the projects to suit local materials and needs. Contributors are encouraged to share their modifications with the community to enhance collective knowledge and innovation.
