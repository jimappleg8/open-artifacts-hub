Precious Plastic is an open-source initiative that empowers individuals worldwide to recycle plastic locally by providing blueprints for machines, instructional videos, and a collaborative platform. Founded by Dave Hakkens, the project aims to reduce plastic waste and promote a circular economy.

- **Project maintainer:** Dave Hakkens and the Precious Plastic team oversee the project's development and global outreach.
- **Collaborative space:** The community connects through the [Precious Plastic Community Platform](https://community.preciousplastic.com/), which offers resources like the [Academy](https://community.preciousplastic.com/academy), [How-to guides](https://community.preciousplastic.com/how-to), and a [Map](https://community.preciousplastic.com/map) to locate nearby recycling spaces. Additionally, discussions and collaborations occur on their [Discord server](https://discord.com/invite/rnx7m4t).
- **Needs Doing list:** The project encourages contributions in machine building, product design, community engagement, and educational outreach. Opportunities are detailed in the [How-to section](https://community.preciousplastic.com/how-to) and through community discussions on the platform.
- **Adapting the project:** All designs and resources are open-source, allowing individuals to modify machines and processes to suit local materials and needs. The [Download Kit](https://community.preciousplastic.com/how-to/download-kit) provides comprehensive materials to get started.
