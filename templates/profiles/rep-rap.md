RepRap (reprap.org) is an open-source project pioneering self-replicating 3D printers. Designed to fabricate most of their own components, RepRap machines enable users to build and customize their printers at low cost. The project fosters innovation, collaboration, and accessibility in 3D printing technology, making it a cornerstone of the maker movement and a catalyst for global creativity and manufacturing independence.
