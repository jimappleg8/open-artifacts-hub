WikiHouse (wikihouse.cc) is an open-source platform revolutionizing the way we design and build homes. It provides free, digital construction kits that enable anyone to download, customize, and fabricate sustainable, low-cost structures using widely accessible materials and tools. WikiHouse empowers communities to create affordable housing solutions while promoting innovation, collaboration, and environmental responsibility in construction.

- **Collaborative space:** A number of ways to contribute are outlined on their [Community page](https://www.wikihouse.cc/community).
