Wikipedia is a free, open-content online encyclopedia collaboratively written and maintained by volunteers worldwide, known as Wikipedians. Founded on January 15, 2001, by Jimmy Wales and Larry Sanger, it has grown to become the largest and most-read reference work in history, consistently ranking among the top ten most visited websites globally. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

- **Project maintainer:** Wikipedia is hosted and supported by the Wikimedia Foundation, a non-profit organization dedicated to fostering the growth of free knowledge.

- **Collaborative space:** Wikipedians collaborate through various channels, including article talk pages, community portals, and specialized groups called WikiProjects, which focus on specific topics or tasks.

- **Needs Doing list:** Contributors can participate in tasks such as article creation, editing for accuracy and neutrality, and patrolling recent changes. Many WikiProjects maintain to-do lists and coordinate efforts to improve content within their scope.

- **Adapting the project:** Wikipedia's content is freely licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike License, allowing anyone to use, share, and adapt the material, provided proper attribution is given and derivative works are licensed under the same terms. Contributors are encouraged to adhere to Wikipedia's core content policies, including verifiability, neutrality, and no original research.
